Why you should attend

EBS Investment Club is hosting founder and investor Jo Bhakdi for an evening on entrepreneurship, technology and the future of investing.

Jo Bhakdi began his career in strategy at WPP and Omnicom before leaving the corporate world to build and back ambitious ventures. Having spent years in Silicon Valley and worked closely with startups, he brings a practical perspective on the technologies and founders most likely to make it to market.

Join Jo Bhakdi for an evening on AI, capital and the opportunities emerging as technology reshapes work and society. Hear how ambitious people can move from watching the future unfold to becoming part of it.
