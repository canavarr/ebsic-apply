Why you should attend

The EBS Investment Club is back with another event. This time we look at investing through the unconventional journey of Maarja Pehk, from the creative industries to the heart of Estonia’s investment world.

Her road into venture capital has been anything but traditional. She studied music, media and the creative industries in the UK and began her career in the London media industry. She then moved to Estonia to support creative businesses, ended up running startup accelerators, and became CEO of Latitude59. After that she returned to London as Head of Investor Relations and FDI for Invest Estonia in the UK. Today she works on the LP side of venture capital at SmartCap, the Estonian state fund manager.

In her keynote, Maarja will share how an unusual background can be an advantage in the investment world, what the Estonian and Baltic venture capital landscape looks like now, and practical advice for students who want to build a career in it.

After the talk there is an open Q&A, so you can ask about investing, startups and leadership.

Come for a fresh view on investing, and on how your own unusual experience can turn into your strongest advantage.
