Learn markets. Build the club. Join a team.

The EBS Investment Club is the student community for people who want to understand investing in practice — through events, public story, and portfolio work. Read the teams, then apply below.

Three ways in

Choose the team you want to join

You will rank a first and second preference on the form. Read each role first — pick the work you actually want to do.

01

Events team

The Events team is responsible for planning and executing the club’s events. This includes inviting speakers, handling event logistics and developing the club’s community. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys organizing, communicating and creating engaging events.

02

Marketing Team

The Marketing Team is responsible for the club’s visibility and communication. This includes managing social media (Instagram, LinkedIn), promoting events and workshops and developing the visual identity. This role is ideal for someone interested in marketing, branding or content creation.

03

Portfolio Management Team

The Portfolio Management Team focuses on investment analysis and strategy. This includes analyzing companies and markets, presenting investment ideas, participating in discussions, and contributing to the development of the club’s investment knowledge. This role suits someone interested in financial analysis, investing and long-term thinking.
