How it works

We'll walk through two ways to approach a stock: fundamental — the business and its numbers — and technical, the chart. Use whichever suits your team, or mix both.

Agree on a stock as a team first, then check the live board before you commit to it. Two teams pitching the same company makes for a duller round, so the board locks each pick to whoever claims it first.

From there you have about an hour to build the case and get your file uploaded. Keep the pitch tight: three minutes, one argument, and the numbers that back it.
